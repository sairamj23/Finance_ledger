Prompt used to build Ledger

Ledger · bill tracking for Team Agnirath · Open the app

Prompt 1

Make a finance management app that uses an OCR feature to extract the necessary details from invoices and store them. Make the UI minimalistic. Do everything, from frontend to backend.

Prompt 2

This app is made keeping Agnirath in mind and the issues we faced. It is to keep track of all the bills. Whenever someone uploads a bill, the things you have to scan are the date of the invoice, the amount spent, the GST number, the address to whom the bill is being sent, and then who paid. If I submit a bill, it should open a form with all the details, just to allow a small manual correction. Then it should ask who paid and whether reimbursement is needed or not needed. You can put those options as checkboxes. These are all the options that should be there. Keep the invoices I give you as example bills in the app rather than random bills like Swiggy and Amazon, because random bills make it look artificial.
